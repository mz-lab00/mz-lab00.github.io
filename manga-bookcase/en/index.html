On your bookshelf

IDEA 01 / Family album

Your child's growth,
one book a year.

Thousands of photos pile up on your phone. Put your favorites into a folder for each year, and you get a shelf that records how your child has grown.

  1. Split by age, keep one spineSplit "Kids Album" into "Age 0", "Age 1" and so on. On the shelf it becomes "Kids Album, 4 vols", in order from age 0.
  2. Make the best smile the coverPick a photo and crop the part you like.
  3. New photos go in "_Unread"Your family sees an "unread" mark and knows right away that new photos were added.
  4. Turn sideways, two at a timeTwo-page view feels like flipping through a paper album.

IDEA 02 / Travel photo book

One book per trip,
your own travelogue.

Add screenshots of tickets and maps and photos of your notes to the pictures you took. It becomes a travel record you can read again and again.

  1. Start file names with "01_", "02_"Pages follow the file name order, so you flip through the trip in order.
  2. Group by destinationSplit "Hokkaido" into "2023 Winter" and "2025 Summer", and trips to the same place share one spine, in order by year.
  3. Keep only a few dozen favoritesA hand-picked selection makes a better photo book than everything you shot.
  4. Scroll through it verticallyScroll from top to bottom to relive the whole trip in one go.

How to start

Get started in 3 steps.

Choose where your books are

Install the app from Google Play, then sign in with Dropbox or choose to read manga on your phone. You can use both later.

Put books in the app's folder

The app creates its own folder for you. From your computer or phone, add the folders, ZIP or PDF files you want as books. On your phone, it's "Internal storage / Documents / Manga Bookcase". In Dropbox, it's the "Manga" folder shown below.

Tap "Rescan"

Tap the Rescan button in the app and your folders appear as books. Then just read.

FAQ

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, every feature is free to use. Ads are shown at the bottom of the screen (you can remove them with a one-time purchase).

Can I remove the ads?

Yes. Buy "Remove ads" once in Settings and ads will no longer be shown (a one-time purchase, no subscription). If you like the app, it's also a way to support its development. After changing phones or reinstalling, tap "Restore purchase" with the same Google account.

Does it work on iPhone or PC?

It's available for Android only at the moment.

Can I use it without Dropbox?

Yes. It also reads folders in your phone's internal storage or on an SD card, so Dropbox is optional. Other cloud services such as Google Drive aren't supported yet. A free Dropbox plan works fine.

Which files can it read?

Folders of JPEG, PNG and WebP images, plus ZIP, CBZ and PDF files. PDFs work for both manga (images) and documents (text), and large PDFs load only the pages you open. RAR and CBR can be turned on in Settings (the whole book is downloaded the first time you open it). Password-protected files aren't supported.

Can I read manga I scanned myself?

Yes. Folders of scanned images, or scans packed into ZIP, CBZ or PDF files, go straight onto your shelf. Keep one folder or file per volume and each volume becomes its own book. You can stay zoomed in while turning pages, so small text in scans is easier to read.

Can I read books I bought from an e-book store?

E-books that only open in the store's own app (copy-protected ones, such as Kindle books) can't be read. The app reads folders of images and ZIP, CBZ, RAR, CBR and PDF files.

How do I put downloaded ZIP or PDF files in the app's folder?

In Google's "Files" app, open the file's menu (⋮), choose "Move to", go to "Internal storage › Documents › Manga Bookcase" and tap "Move here". Then tap "Rescan" in the app. (A step-by-step guide with pictures is available in Japanese.)

How do I hide a folder from the shelf?

Put it in the "_Excluded" folder and it won't appear on your bookshelf.

Are my images sent anywhere?

No. Images are loaded directly from Dropbox or your phone's folders to your device and are never sent to MZ lab's or anyone else's servers.
